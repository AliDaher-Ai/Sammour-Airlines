Flights To Ottawa

Ottawa, the capital of Canada, is a charming city known for its beautiful blend of history, culture, and natural beauty. Situated along the Ottawa River, it features landmarks like the iconic Parliament Hill and the scenic Rideau Canal. It's a city where modernity meets tradition, making it a captivating destination for travelers.

Flights to Canada

Flights to Canada take you to a land where vast landscapes, rich history, and diverse culture come together in the most awe-inspiring way. From the iconic Rocky Mountains to the lively streets of Toronto, Canada offers a journey through nature and urban sophistication. The cosmopolitan city of Montreal, with its blend of European charm and modern flair, and the peaceful lakeside towns of Ontario invite visitors to explore the country’s unique mix of wilderness and culture.

Canada’s history is a rich tapestry woven from Indigenous traditions, French and British colonial influences, and the stories of early pioneers. Whether it's exploring the historic Old Quebec, wandering through the charming streets of Halifax, or enjoying the beauty of the Thousand Islands, Canada offers something for every type of traveler.

The country’s natural beauty is as diverse as its history, from the rugged coastlines of the Maritimes to the pristine lakes and dense forests of British Columbia. The vast national parks and majestic mountains make Canada an ideal destination for nature lovers, outdoor adventurers, and wildlife enthusiasts.

A journey to Canada is one of exploration and excitement, where history, culture, and natural beauty await at every turn.

Trip inspiration

What to do in Canada

Explore the Majestic Rocky Mountains

The Canadian Rockies offer some of the most breathtaking views in the world. Whether you’re hiking through Banff and Jasper National Parks, skiing at Whistler, or simply soaking in the beauty of turquoise lakes like Lake Louise, the Rockies are a must-see for nature lovers and adventure seekers.

Discover the Charm of Old Quebec

Step back in time with a visit to Old Quebec, a UNESCO World Heritage site. Walk along the cobblestone streets, explore historic landmarks like Château Frontenac, and experience the European atmosphere that makes this city one of Canada’s most unique and charming destinations.

Experience the Vibrancy of Toronto’s Cultural Scene

Toronto is Canada's largest and most multicultural city, brimming with art, food, and music. From visiting iconic spots like the CN Tower and Royal Ontario Museum to exploring diverse neighborhoods like Chinatown and Little Italy, Toronto offers endless opportunities to experience a rich cultural tapestry.

Where to eat in Ottowa

Indulge in Fine Dining at Beckta

Known for its elegant atmosphere and exceptional fine dining, Beckta offers a modern take on classic Canadian cuisine. With an impressive wine list and creative dishes made from locally sourced ingredients, it's perfect for a special night out in Ottawa.

Experience Culinary Innovation at Atelier

For a truly unique dining experience, Atelier serves up contemporary cuisine with an innovative twist. Their tasting menu, which changes regularly, is a highlight for foodies looking to explore bold flavors and inventive presentation in an intimate, upscale setting.

Savor Fresh Seafood at The Whalesbone

If you’re a seafood lover, The Whalesbone is a must-visit. Known for its fresh, sustainable seafood and cozy atmosphere, this restaurant offers a menu that showcases the best of Canada’s ocean bounty, including oysters, fish, and shellfish.

Places to stay in Ottowa

Stay in Luxury at Fairmont Château Laurier

A landmark in Ottawa, the Fairmont Château Laurier combines historic charm with modern luxury. Located just steps from Parliament Hill, it offers elegant rooms, exceptional service, and stunning views of the Rideau Canal.

Experience Modern Comfort at Andaz Ottawa Byward Market

This trendy boutique hotel is the perfect blend of comfort and style. Situated in the vibrant Byward Market, Andaz offers spacious rooms, contemporary design, and easy access to the city's best dining, shopping, and attractions.

Relax in Style at The Westin Ottawa

With its prime location in downtown Ottawa, The Westin is ideal for both business and leisure travelers. Guests can enjoy luxurious amenities, including an indoor pool and modern fitness center, as well as breathtaking views of the city skyline and Parliament Hill.
