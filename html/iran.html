Flights to Iran

Flights to Iran take you to a land rich in history, culture, and stunning landscapes. From the ancient ruins of Persepolis to the majestic gardens of Shiraz, the architectural beauty of Isfahan’s Naqsh-e Jahan Square, and the bustling bazaars of Tehran, Iran is a country full of unforgettable landmarks.

It’s a place where past and present intertwine, offering both ancient wonders like the Achaemenid Empire’s treasures and modern attractions such as the towering Milad Tower.

Iran’s cities are vibrant and varied, with each offering its own unique charm – whether it’s the historic charm of Yazd’s wind towers, the cozy cafes of Tabriz, or the luxurious palaces of Tehran. The country is also blessed with diverse natural beauty, from the lush Caspian Sea coast to the desert landscapes surrounding Yazd and the snow-capped peaks of the Alborz Mountains.

The country’s rich cultural heritage is reflected in its UNESCO World Heritage Sites, stunning Persian carpets, and the welcoming hospitality of its people. From the serene beauty of Lake Urmia to the historic wonders of Kashan’s ancient houses, Iran offers endless treasures to discover.Iran's timeless beauty is complemented by its rich cultural festivals, where music, dance, and poetry come together to celebrate centuries of tradition.

From the lively Nowruz celebrations in spring to the soulful rhythms of Persian music, there's always a reason to immerse yourself in the country’s vibrant cultural scene.

Trip inspiration

What to do in Iran

Exploring the Wonders of Persepolis

Persepolis, the ancient capital of the Achaemenid Empire, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site near Shiraz. The well-preserved ruins, including grand gateways and towering columns, offer a glimpse into Iran’s rich history and architecture, making it a must-see for history enthusiasts.

Discovering the Majestic Beauty of Isfahan

Isfahan is known for its stunning architecture, highlighted by Imam Square and the Shah Mosque. Visitors can explore its beautiful bridges, vibrant bazaars, and enjoy traditional Persian cuisine in a city full of history and culture, leaving a lasting impression of Persian grandeur.

Enjoying the Serene Nature of Northern Iran

Northern Iran offers lush landscapes, including the Caspian Sea coast and the Alborz mountains. Ideal for hiking, skiing, and relaxing in charming villages, this region showcases Iran’s natural beauty and tranquil atmosphere, offering a peaceful retreat from bustling city life.

Where to eat in Tehran

Enjoying Traditional Persian Flavors at Shandiz Mashad

Shandiz Mashad in Tehran offers an authentic taste of Persian cuisine, specializing in Shandiz kebab—tender lamb grilled to perfection. The restaurant’s inviting atmosphere and range of traditional dishes make it a must-visit for those seeking rich Persian flavors.

Indulging in Exotic Street Food at Tajrish Bazaar

ajrish Bazaar is a lively spot for street food lovers, where you can sample falafel, samosas, and kebabs from local vendors. The bustling market also offers refreshing desserts like faloodeh and baklava, adding to the vibrant culinary experience.

Experiencing Fine Dining at Divan Restaurant

Divan Restaurant provides a refined dining experience, blending modern techniques with traditional Persian flavors. Signature dishes like tahchin and ghormeh sabzi are presented with a contemporary twist, making it a perfect choice for elegant dining in Tehran.

Places to stay in Tehran

Experience Luxury at the Espinas Palace Hotel

The Espinas Palace Hotel offers a five-star experience with luxurious rooms, a spa, and fine dining. Located in northern Tehran, it provides stunning views of the Alborz Mountains and is close to shopping areas and cultural sites, ensuring a memorable stay.

Relax in Comfort at the Laleh International Hotel

The Laleh International Hotel is centrally located and offers a range of rooms, a swimming pool, gym, and delicious restaurants. With easy access to Laleh Park and museums, it's an ideal choice for both business and leisure travelers.

Stay in Style at the Parsian Kowsar Hotel

The Parsian Kowsar Hotel combines comfort and affordability in the heart of Tehran. Its cozy rooms and prime location near major attractions like Golestan Palace make it a great choice for those seeking convenience and comfort.
