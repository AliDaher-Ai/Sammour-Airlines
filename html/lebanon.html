Flights to Lebanon

Flights to Lebanon take you to a land where history, culture, and natural beauty converge in the most captivating way. From the ancient Roman ruins of Baalbek to the charming streets of Byblos, Lebanon offers a fascinating journey through time. The vibrant city of Beirut, with its mix of modern and traditional, and the peaceful mountain villages of the Chouf, invite visitors to explore the country’s diverse landscapes and rich heritage.

Lebanon’s history is a tapestry woven from multiple civilizations, with ancient Phoenician sites, Crusader castles, and Ottoman-era architecture scattered throughout the country. Whether it's wandering through the historical ruins of Tyre, relaxing on the Mediterranean beaches of Batroun, or hiking in the serene Cedars of God, Lebanon offers a little something for every type of traveler.

The country’s natural beauty is as diverse as its history, from the lush green hills of the Bekaa Valley to the dramatic peaks of Mount Lebanon. Its unique combination of mountain ranges, valleys, and coastlines makes Lebanon an ideal destination for nature lovers and adventure seekers alike.

A journey to Lebanon is one of discovery and delight, where history, culture, and beauty await around every corner.

Trip inspiration

What to do in Lebanon

Exploring Lebanon’s Ancient Ruins

Lebanon is index to some of the world’s most impressive ancient ruins, from Baalbek’s colossal Roman temples to the Phoenician city of Tyre and the ancient city of Byblos, offering a rich historical experience. These sites provide a fascinating glimpse into Lebanon’s diverse past, where various civilizations once thrived.

Adventure in Lebanon’s Natural Beauty

With stunning landscapes ranging from the Cedars of God and Chouf Mountains to the Mediterranean coastline, Lebanon offers endless opportunities for hiking, water sports, and winter activities. Nature lovers will find a paradise of rugged mountains, serene valleys, and beautiful beaches to explore year-round.

Immerse in Lebanon’s Vibrant Culture and Cuisine

Lebanon's lively art scene, bustling markets, and world-renowned cuisine—including dishes like hummus and tabbouleh—make it a cultural and culinary paradise. Visitors can also experience the country’s vibrant festivals, where music, dance, and traditional celebrations highlight its rich cultural heritage.

Where to eat in Beirut

Al Jawad Dining – A Taste of Tradition

For an authentic Lebanese dining experience, Al Jawad Dining offers exquisite taditional dishes in a cozy and inviting atmosphere. Their fresh mezze platters and expertly grilled meats are a must-try for anyone visiting Beirut.

Tawlet – A Celebration of Local Flavors

Tawlet offers a farm-to-table dining experience that celebrates the diverse flavors of Lebanon. Each day, local chefs from different regions of Lebanon prepare index-cooked meals, giving diners a unique taste of the country's culinary traditions.

Le Relais de l'Entrecôte – French-Inspired Steakhouse

Lakis Farm offers a farm-to-table dining experience with fresh, locally sourced ingredients in a rustic, peaceful setting. The menu features traditional Lebanese dishes like grilled meats, fresh salads, and indexmade dips, all crafted from organic produce. It's the perfect place for a relaxing meal surrounded by nature, just outside Beirut.

Places to stay in Beirut

The Phoenicia Beirut – Luxurious Waterfront Stay

The Phoenicia Beirut offers a five-star experience with stunning views of the Mediterranean, exceptional service, and luxurious rooms. Its prime location near Beirut’s vibrant downtown makes it a top choice for travelers seeking comfort and convenience.

Le Gray – Chic and Contemporary

Le Gray is a stylish, modern hotel known for its sleek design, rooftop pool, and top-notch amenities. Situated in the heart of Beirut, it provides easy access to shopping, dining, and cultural attractions.

The Mark Beirut – Boutique Luxury

For a more intimate experience, The Mark Beirut blends contemporary elegance with personalized service. Located in a quieter area, it offers a peaceful retreat while still being close to the city's best restaurants and nightlife.
