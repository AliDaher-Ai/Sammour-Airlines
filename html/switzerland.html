Flights to Switzerland

Flights to Switzerland transport you to a land of breathtaking natural beauty and cultural richness. From the towering Swiss Alps to the serene lakeside towns, the country offers a stunning landscape.

Cities like Zurich and Geneva blend modernity with historical charm, while destinations like Lucerne and Interlaken showcase Switzerland's serene, picturesque side.

Switzerland is a place where nature and culture meet, with famous landmarks like the Matterhorn and the medieval castles of Chillon, as well as contemporary attractions like the Swiss National Park. The country is a paradise for outdoor enthusiasts, offering world-class skiing, hiking, and cycling through some of the most beautiful landscapes on Earth

Switzerland’s rich heritage shines through its art, music, and cuisine, from the culinary delights of Swiss chocolate and fondue to the vibrant festivals celebrated throughout the year. Whether exploring the charming streets of Bern, visiting historic villages, or enjoying the peaceful beauty of the Swiss countryside, Switzerland is a place that offers endless discovery.

Trip inspiration

What to do in Switzerland

Explore the Swiss Alps

Take a scenic hike or enjoy world-class skiing in the Swiss Alps. Whether you’re an avid skier or a nature lover, the towering peaks, lush meadows, and stunning views offer unforgettable outdoor adventures year-round.

Visit the Historic Château de Chillon

ocated on the shores of Lake Geneva, this medieval castle is one of Switzerland’s most iconic landmarks. Wander through its well-preserved rooms, discover its fascinating history, and enjoy breathtaking views of the surrounding mountains and lake.

Discover the Charm of Lucerne

Stroll along Lucerne’s picturesque streets, visit the Chapel Bridge, and take a boat tour on Lake Lucerne. This charming city, with its old-town vibe and surrounding natural beauty, offers a perfect blend of history, art, and serene landscapes.

Where to eat in Bern

Dining in Elegance at Kornhauskeller

Located in a stunning historic cellar, Kornhauskeller offers a mix of Swiss and Mediterranean cuisine. Its elegant setting and delicious dishes make it a must-visit for both locals and tourists. The charming ambiance adds a unique touch to every meal.

Scenic Dining at Rosengarten Restaurant

For a meal with a view, head to Rosengarten, where you can enjoy Swiss specialties while overlooking the city of Bern and the beautiful Aare River. It’s the perfect spot for a relaxed dining experience. Don’t miss their exceptional wine list to pair with your meal.

Craft Beer and Comfort Food at Altes Tramdepot

Located near the Bear Park, Altes Tramdepot is a popular brewery and restaurant serving hearty Swiss fare. Try their locally brewed beer and enjoy a casual meal with a great atmosphere. The vibrant setting makes it a fun spot to unwind with friends.

Places to stay in Bern

Timeless Luxury at Badrutt’s Palace Hotel (St. Moritz)

A luxurious icon in the Swiss Alps, Badrutt’s Palace offers impeccable service, world-class amenities, and stunning views of Lake St. Moritz. With its blend of historic charm and modern comfort, it's perfect for those seeking an unforgettable mountain retreat.

Elevated Elegance at The Dolder Grand (Zurich)

This 5-star hotel combines traditional Swiss hospitality with contemporary luxury. Set atop a hill, The Dolder Grand provides breathtaking views of Zurich and the surrounding Alps, along with a spa, fine dining, and exquisite art collections, making it an ideal stay for relaxation and sophistication.

Alpine Retreat at Kulm Hotel (Engadine Valley)

Nestled in the picturesque Engadine Valley, Kulm Hotel is renowned for its elegant interiors and breathtaking Alpine scenery. Offering ski-in/ski-out access and top-notch wellness facilities, it’s perfect for those seeking both adventure and relaxation in a charming mountain setting.
